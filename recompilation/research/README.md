# Recompilation Research

This directory contains technical investigations, research findings, analysis, and documentation relating specifically to recompilation of the Ocarina of Time remake.

Research may include investigations into recompilation-related architecture, technical characteristics, implementation approaches, behavior, compatibility, and other relevant topics.

## Documentation Guidelines

Research should aim to document the investigation and its findings in a way that others can understand, evaluate, and reproduce where applicable.

Where relevant, research documentation should include:

- **Objective:** The question or subject being investigated.
- **Methodology:** The approach, conditions, and procedures used during the investigation.
- **Observations:** Directly observed behavior or collected information.
- **Findings:** Conclusions supported by the observations or evidence.
- **Evidence and References:** Supporting materials and sources.
- **Environment:** Relevant software versions, configurations, and conditions.
- **Limitations:** Uncertainties, assumptions, or unresolved questions.
- **Verification Status:** Whether findings have been verified, partially verified, or remain unverified.

Research should distinguish direct observations from interpretations, assumptions, and hypotheses.

Additional organizational structures may be established as the scope of research develops.
