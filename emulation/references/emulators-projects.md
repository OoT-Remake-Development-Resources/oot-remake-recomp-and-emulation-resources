# Switch 2 Emulator Projects

This document catalogs emulator projects and forks relevant to running the Nintendo Switch 2 version of *The Legend of Zelda: Ocarina of Time* on other hardware.

The projects listed here are independently maintained and vary in their development status, compatibility, supported hardware, and intended scope.

## Projects

### Lichii

- **Type:** Nintendo Switch 2 Emulator
- **Source:** https://github.com/zortos293/Lichii
- **Description:** An emulator project with community development focused on Nintendo Switch 2 compatibility. Various forks and related builds have explored compatibility with the Ocarina of Time remake.
- **Status:** Independent development

### Fritchii

- **Type:** Nintendo Switch 2 Emulator / Game-focused Fork
- **Source:** https://fritchii.io/
- **Description:** A Lichii-derived emulator fork with settings and optimizations specifically aimed at running the Ocarina of Time remake.
- **Status:** Independent development

### Nyamu

- **Type:** Nintendo Switch 2 Emulator / Hardware-specific Fork
- **Source:** https://github.com/Spikol02/Nyamu
- **Description:** An emulator fork focused specifically on the Ocarina of Time remake on AMD Radeon GPUs, with hardware-specific performance and compatibility modifications.
- **Status:** Independent development

### Eglantine

- **Type:** Nintendo Switch 2 Emulator
- **Source:** https://github.com/sylvieistaken/selevet-eglantine
- **Description:** An experimental emulator project derived from the Eden ecosystem, with community development targeting Nintendo Switch 2 emulation.
- **Status:** Independent development

### Selevet Eglantine

- **Type:** Nintendo Switch 2 Emulator / Fork
- **Source:** https://github.com/sylvieistaken/selevet-eglantine
- **Description:** A community emulator fork associated with experimental Switch 2 compatibility work. Its history and compatibility information may be useful for documenting early developments.
- **Status:** Historical / development status should be verified

### Guarana

- **Type:** Nintendo Switch 2 Emulator
- **Source:** https://github.com/karimalsalah/guarana
- **Description:** An open-source emulator project with compatibility work and additional features targeting Nintendo Switch 2 games, including the Ocarina of Time remake.
- **Status:** Independent development
