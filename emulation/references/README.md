# Emulation References

This directory contains references to external projects, repositories, documentation, publications, articles, tools, and other resources relevant to emulation of the Ocarina of Time remake.

References may include independently maintained projects, technical documentation, relevant research, development resources, and other materials that may support investigations or development efforts.

## Reference Documentation

Whenever possible, references should include:

- **Name:** The name of the resource.
- **Link:** A link to its original source.
- **Description:** A brief description of its content or purpose.
- **Relevance:** Why the resource may be useful to emulation research or development.

References should link to original sources whenever possible and avoid reproducing third-party material without appropriate permission.

Inclusion in this directory does not imply affiliation, endorsement, compatibility, or verification by this repository or its contributors.

This directory may be expanded or reorganized as additional resources are identified.
